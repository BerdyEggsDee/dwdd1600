Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible and respectful way a person behaves when using technology and the internet.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, computers, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy.

An AUP is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use an organization's technology and network resources. Schools, universities, workplaces, and other organizations commonly have AUPs.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, work, or creations without giving them proper credit and presenting them as your own.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any action that gives a student an unfair academic advantage or misrepresents their work or knowledge.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

Use AI responsibly, follow your school's policies, disclose AI use when required, verify its information, and don't use it to cheat.
