Introduction to Shiny Pokémon

Shiny Pokémon are extremely rare variants of Pokémon that have a different color scheme from their normal appearances. First introduced in Pokémon Gold and Silver, Shiny Pokémon have become a favorite challenge for trainers looking to find these rare variations. While they are no stronger than their regular counterparts, their rarity makes encountering one an exciting achievement for any Pokémon fan.

How to Find and Catch a Shiny Pokémon

  1. Choose the shiny you want to hunt. Decide which Shiny Pokémon you want to find and choose a hunting method that works in your game.
  2. Prepare for the hunt. Stock up on the various Poké Ball types listed below and make sure your Pokémon have useful moves such as False Swipe or status moves.
  3. Start your hunt. Use your chosen method to encounter Pokémon and continue searching until a Shiny appears.
  4. Identify the Shiny. Look for the Pokémon's alternate coloration and any Shiny animation or sound effects your game provides.
  5. Weaken the Pokémon. Carefully lower its HP without knocking it out. False Swipe is especially useful for this.
  6. Catch the Shiny! Throw Poké Balls until the Shiny Pokémon is caught. Consider using a special Poké Ball that complements its appearance.
  7. Celebrate your new Shiny! Add it to your collection and record your hunt's results in your Shiny Dex.

Useful Items

Shiny Charm
An important Key Item that increases the odds of encountering Shiny Pokémon. It is one of the most useful items for dedicated Shiny Hunters.
Ultra Balls
Ultra Balls have the highest base catch rate and are useful when catching Pokémon that don't satisfy conditions for more effective ball types.
Quick Ball
A Poké Ball with a high catch rate when used at the beginning of an encounter, making it useful for quickly catching a Shiny Pokémon.
Timer Ball
A Poké Ball that becomes more effective as an encounter lasts longer, making it useful for difficult-to-catch Shiny Pokémon.
False Swipe
A move that leaves a Pokémon with at least 1 HP, making it useful for weakening a Shiny Pokémon without accidentally knocking it out.
Pokémon with Synchronize
In games where Synchronize affects Pokémon natures, having a Pokémon with the desired nature can help you obtain a Shiny Pokémon with that nature.
Pokémon with Sweet Scent
A Pokémon that knows Sweet Scent can be used to quickly trigger encounters in games where the move can initiate wild Pokémon encounters.
Adrenaline Orb
An item used in the Alola games to encourage wild Pokémon to call for help, making it useful for hunting Shiny Pokémon through chain encounters.
Repel
Repels prevent lower-level wild Pokémon from appearing for a limited number of steps. They can be useful for manipulating which Pokémon appear during certain hunts.
