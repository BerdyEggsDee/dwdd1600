Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous people who signed the Declaration of Independence were:

Thomas Jefferson
Primary author of the Declaration; later became the 3rd U.S. president.
John Adams
Major advocate for independence; later became the 2nd U.S. president.
Benjamin Franklin
Famous scientist, inventor, diplomat, and statesman; one of the oldest signers.
John Hancock
President of the Continental Congress; known for his famously large signature.
Samuel Adams
Revolutionary leader and cousin of John Adams.
John Witherspoon
The only active college president to sign; president of the College of New Jersey, now Princeton.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton
One of the last surviving signers and the only Roman Catholic signer.
Richard Henry Lee
Introduced the resolution for independence that led to the Declaration.

Each of these individuals played a significant role in the founding of the United States and is remembered for their courage in signing the Declaration, an act considered treasonous by the British Crown.
